Ramping Reps roster & weighting

In RR AE Target weight Booked (RR) Held (RR) Hold rate Held, all sources (30d) Effective weight

Target weight is the % share of round-robin deals an AE should receive within their own pool; the spinner uses deficit-based fair queueing so actual assignments converge to these targets over many spins rather than drifting with short-run randomness. The Core and Ramping Reps pools are weighted completely independently — booking in one never affects the other's queue. Booked / Held (RR) counts only deals assigned through this tool. Booking increments automatically on each spin; Held increments automatically once the hourly Salesforce sync sees the linked opp reach Discovery — there's no manual override, since self-reported "held" status defeats the point of weighting off real Salesforce data. Held, all sources (30d) is informational only — every Opportunity that reached Discovery in the trailing 30 days, regardless of how it was booked — and is never factored into assignment.
